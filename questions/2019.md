# Math Questions — 2019

1. (a) Show A=2i+j−3k, B=i−4k, C=4i+3j−k linearly dependent; relation and terminal points collinear.
   (b) Find unit tangent vector at t=2 on x=t²+1,y=4t−3,z=2t²−6t.
   (c) Show E⃗=r⃗/r² irrotational; find φ, E⃗=−∇φ, φ(a)=0, a>0.
2. (a) If F⃗=(2x²−3z)i−2xyj−4xk, evaluate ∭V(∇×F⃗)dV, V bounded x=0,y=0,z=0,2x+2y+z=4.
   (b) Evaluate ∬S A⃗·n̂ds where A⃗=yi+2xj−zk and S plane 2x+y=6, first octant cut by z=4.
3. (a) What is inverse? Find inverse [[1,3,3],[1,4,3],[1,1,3]].
   (b) Examine consistency x+y+4z=6; 3x+2y−2z=9; 5x+y+2z=13, and solve.
4. (a) Transform A=[[0,1,−3,−1],[1,0,1,1],[3,1,0,2],[1,1,−2,0]] into normal form and find rank.
   (b) Verify Cayley-Hamilton theorem for A=[[2,−1,1],[−1,2,−1],[1,−1,2]].
5. (a) Define DE. Form DE corresponding to y=ae^(2x)+be^(−2x)+ce^x, a,b,c parameters.
   (b) Solve (2x−y+1)dx+(2y−x−1)dy=0.
   (c) Find general solution dy/dx+2xy=2e^(−x²).
6. (a) What is integrating factor? Find IF of ydx+(3+3x−y)dy=0 and solve.
   (b) Find current from Ldi/dt+Ri=E₀sinωt, L,R,E₀ constants.
   (c) Find general solution dy/dx=x²y³−xy.
