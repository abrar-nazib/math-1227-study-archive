# Math 1227 Questions — 2022

### Q.1

(a) Forces of magnitudes 5 and 3 units acting in the direction \(6\hat i+2\hat j+3\hat k\) and \(3\hat i-2\hat j+6\hat k\) respectively act on a particle which is displaced from the point \((2,2,-1)\) to \((4,3,1)\). Find the work done by the forces.

(b) In what direction from the point \((1,3,2)\) is the directional derivative of \(\phi=2xz-y^2\) a maximum? What is the magnitude of this maximum?

(c) Show that \(\vec F=(2xy+z^3)\hat i+x^2\hat j+3xz^2\hat k\) is a conservative force field. Also, find the scalar potential.

### Q.2

(a) If \(\vec F=(2x+y)\hat i+(3y-x)\hat j\), then evaluate \(\int_C\vec F\cdot d\vec r\) where \(C\) is the curve in the \(xy\) plane consisting of the straight lines from \((0,0)\) to \((2,0)\) and then to \((3,2)\).

(b) Evaluate \(\iint_S\phi\hat n\,dS\) where \(\phi=\frac{3}{8}xyz\) and \(S\) is the surface of the cylinder \(x^2+y^2=16\) included in the first octant between \(z=0\) and \(z=5\).

### Q.3

(a) State and prove divergence theorem.

(b) Verify Green’s theorem in the plane for \(\oint_C(3x^2-8y^2)dx+(4y-6xy)dy\), where \(C\) is the boundary of the region defined by \(y=\sqrt{x}\) and \(y=x^2\).

### Q.4

(a) Define symmetric and skew-Hermitian matrix with example.

(b) Show that every square matrix can be uniquely expressed as the sum of a symmetric and skew-symmetric matrices.

(c) Define rank of a matrix. Reduce the matrix \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2&3&-1&-1\\1&-1&-2&-4\\3&1&3&-2\\6&3&0&-7\end{pmatrix}\) into normal form and hence find its rank.

### Q.5

(a) Determine for what values of \(\lambda\) and \(\mu\) the system \(x+y+z=6\), \(x+2y+3z=10\), \(x+2y+\lambda z=\mu\) has: (i) no solution, (ii) unique solution, (iii) infinite number of solutions.

(b) Find the eigen values and the corresponding eigen vectors of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&-1\\1&2&1\\2&2&3\end{pmatrix}\).

### Q.6

(a) Define a differential equation. Give examples. Mention some applications of differential equations.

(b) Solve the initial value problem: \(x\sin y\,dx+(x^2+1)\cos y\,dy=0,\ y(0)=\frac{\pi}{2}\).

(c) Find the constant \(A\) such that \(\left(\frac{Ay}{x^3}+\frac{y}{x^2}\right)dx+\left(\frac{1}{x^2}-\frac{1}{x}\right)dy=0\) is exact and solve the resulting equation.

### Q.7

(a) Identify and solve \(xy-\frac{dy}{dx}=y^3e^{-x^2}\).

(b) Find the complete solution of \(y''-6y'+25y=0;\ y(0)=-3,\ y'(0)=-1\).

### Q.8

Solve completely: (a) \((D^2-4)y=e^x-\sin x\); (b) \((D^2-4D+4)y=x^2+e^x\); (c) \((D^2+1)y=x\sin x\).
