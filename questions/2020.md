# Math Questions — 2020

### Section A

1. (a) Determine whether r₁=2i−j+k, r₂=i+3j−2k, r₃=−2i+j−3k and r₄=3i+2j+5k are linearly independent or dependent.
   (b) Determine volume of tetrahedron whose adjacent edges are A⃗=2i+j−3k, B⃗=i−4k, C⃗=4i+3j−k.
   (c) Show F⃗=e^x cosz i+2yj−e^x sinz k is irrotational. Find scalar φ such that F⃗=∇φ.
2. (a) Show F⃗=(2xy+z³)i+x²j+3xz²k is a conservative force field. Find scalar potential.
   (b) If F⃗=4xzi−y²j+yzk, evaluate ∬S F⃗·n̂ds where S is surface of cube x=0,1;y=0,1;z=0,1.
3. (a) State and prove divergence theorem.
   (b) Evaluate ∭V(2x+y)dV, where V is bounded by cylinder z=4−x² and planes x=0,y=0,y=2,z=0.
4. (a) What is orthogonal matrix? If A,B orthogonal, show AB also orthogonal.
   (b) Express [[−1,7,1],[2,3,4],[5,0,5]] as sum of symmetric and skew-symmetric matrices.
   (c) Find inverse A=[[1,3,3],[1,4,3],[1,3,4]].

### Section B

5. (a) Define rank. Reduce A=[[1,−1,2,−3],[4,1,0,2],[0,3,0,4],[0,1,0,2]] to normal form; find rank.
   (b) Find characteristic roots/vectors A=[[2,√2],[√2,1]].
6. (a) What is DE? Obtain DE of family xy=ae^x+be^(−x), a,b parameters.
   (b) Solve y²+x²dy/dx=xy dy/dx. [as printed]
   (c) Find IF of (3x²y⁴+2xy)dx+(2x³y³−x²)dy=0. Hence solve.
7. Solve: (i)(D²+2D+1)y=e^x+e^(−x); (ii)(D²+1)y=x sinx; (iii)(D²−4D+4)y=x²e^(2x); D=d/dx.
8. (a) Variation of parameters: (x−1)D²y−xDy+y=(x−1)², D=d/dx.
   (b) Solve x²y''+xy'+y=x, x=1,y=0,y'=0.
