# First-order ordinary differential equation questions

Questions are grouped by paper year, newest first. This collection includes first-order equations, exactness and integrating-factor theory, first-order applications, and formation from one-parameter families. General differential-equation definitions are included as background. The wording and notation below follow the existing yearly transcriptions, including their uncertainty markers; consult the linked paper and original scan when needed. Higher-order equations and formation from multiple-parameter families are excluded. The undated paper is listed separately at the end.
