# Math Questions — 2015

### Section A

1. (a) Define: Square matrix, Diagonal matrix, Upper triangular matrix, Symmetric matrix, Hermitian matrix.
   (b) Prove matrix multiplication is distributive with respect to matrix addition.
   (c) What is transpose of a matrix? For matrices A and B prove (AB)' = B'A'.
2. (a) Find x,y,z and a satisfying [[x+3,2y+x],[z−1,4a−6]]=[[0,−7],[3,2a]].
   (b) Prove a square matrix A possesses an inverse iff A is non-singular.
   (c) Find rank of A=[[1,2,−1,3],[3,4,0,−1],[−1,0,−2,7]].
3. (a) Find inverse of A=[[0,1,2],[1,2,3],[3,1,1]].
   (b) Determine α,β,γ when [[0,2β,γ],[α,β,−γ],[α,−β,γ]] is orthogonal.
   (c) Show diagonal elements of Hermitian matrix are real and of skew Hermitian matrix are either zero or pure imaginary.
4. (a) Show A=2i+j−3k, B=i−4k and C=4i+3j−k are linearly dependent. Determine a relation and hence show terminal points are collinear.
   (b) Find angle between x y² z=3x²+2 and 3x²−y²+2z=1 at (1,−2,1). [Source-faded]
   (c) Find a,b,c so V=(x+2y+az)i+(bx−3y−z)j+(4x+cy+2z)k is irrotational.

### Section B

5. (a) Solve: (xy+y²)dx+(xy²−x²)dy=0.
   (b) Define integrating factor. Find IF of dy/dx+y=3x²−3x, hence solve.
   (c) Find general solution dy/dx + y/(2x) = 1/y, y(1)=2.
6. Solve: (i) (D²+9)y=x²+x+1; (ii) (D²−4D+4)y=e^x+cos2x+x²; (iii) y'''−4y''+3y'=3e^x cos x+2xe^(3x).
7. (a) Solve d²y/dx²+y=tan x. (b) Solve d²y/dx²−2dy/dx−3y=2e^x−10sin x.
8. (a) A parachutist is falling at 55 ms⁻¹ when parachute opens. Air resistance is Wv²/25 N, W total weight. Find speed as a function of time.
   (b) L di/dt+Ri=E(t). Find current when E(t)=E₀ and initial current i₀, R,L constants.
