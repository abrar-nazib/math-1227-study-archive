# Math 1227 Questions — 2024

### Q.1

(a) Find an equation for the plane perpendicular to \(\vec A=2\hat i+3\hat j+6\hat k\) and passing through the terminal point to the vector \(\vec B=\hat i+5\hat j+3\hat k\).

(b) Show that \(\vec E=\frac{\vec r}{r^2}\) is conservative. Find a scalar function \(\phi\) such that \(\vec E=-\nabla\phi\) and \(\phi(a)=0\), where \(a>0\).

(c) Evaluate \(\int_C\vec A\cdot d\vec r\) along the curve \(x^2+y^2=1,\ Z=1\) in the positive direction of \((0,1,1)\) to \((1,0,1)\) if \(\vec A=(yz+zx)\hat i+xz\hat j+(xy+2z)\hat k\).

### Q.2

(a) Evaluate \(\iint_S(\nabla\times\vec F)\cdot\hat n\,ds\) where \(\vec F=(x+2y)\hat i-3z\hat j+x\hat k\) and \(S\) is the surface of \(2x+y+2z=6\) bounded by \(x=0,x=1,y=0,y=2\).

(b) If \(\vec F=(2x^2-3z)\hat i-2xy\hat j-4x\hat k\), then evaluate \(\iiint_V(\nabla\times\vec F)dV\) where \(V\) is the closed region bounded by \(x=0,y=0,z=0,2x+2y+z=4\).

### Q.3

(a) State and prove divergence theorem.

(b) Verify Stoke’s theorem for \(\vec F=xz\hat i-y\hat j+x^2yz\hat k\), where \(s\) is the surface of the region bounded by \(x=0,y=0,z=0,2x+y+2z=8\) which is not included in the \(xz\) plane.

### Q.4

(a) What is the adjoint of a matrix? Prove that the adjoint of a symmetric matrix is itself symmetric.

(b) Find the symmetric and skew symmetric part of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2&3&4\\4&3&1\\1&2&4\end{pmatrix}\).

(c) Find the Hermitian part of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2+3i&1-i&2+i\\3&4+3i&5\\1&1+i&2i\end{pmatrix}\).

### Q.5

(a) Find the eigen values and the corresponding eigen vectors of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&-2\\-1&2&1\\0&1&-1\end{pmatrix}\).

(b) Solve by matrix method: \(x-y+2z=3\), \(x+2y+3z=5\), \(3x-4y-5z=-13\).

### Q.6

(a) Define rank of a matrix. Reduce \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2&3&-1&-1\\1&-1&-2&-4\\3&1&3&-2\\6&3&0&-7\end{pmatrix}\) into normal form and hence find its rank.

(b) Define differential equation. Also mention the uses of differential equation.

(c) Solve \((y^2e^{xy^2}+4x^3)dx+(2xye^{xy^2}-3y^2)dy=0\).

### Q.7

(a) What is exact differential equation? Find the general solution of \((12y+4x^3+6x^2)dx+3(x+xy^2)dy=0\).

(b) Solve \(\frac{dy}{dx}+x\sin2y=x^3\cos^2y\).

(c) Find the integrating factor and hence find the general solution of \(y\ln y\,dx+(x-\ln y)dy=0\).

### Q.8

(a) Solve \((x^3D^3+2xD-2)y=x^2\ln x+3x\).

(b) Find the current \(i\) from \(L\frac{di}{dt}+Ri=E\sin2t\), where \(L,R,E\) are constants subject to \(i=0\) when \(t=0\).
