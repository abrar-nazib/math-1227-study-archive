# Math Questions — 2018

### Section A

1. (a) What is an orthogonal matrix? Show [[cosα,sinα],[−sinα,cosα]] is orthogonal.
   (b) Determine by normal form rank of A=[[1,0,2,1],[0,1,−2,1],[1,−1,4,0],[2,2,8,0]].
   (c) If a matrix has an inverse, show inverse is unique.
2. (a) Apply test of rank to examine whether 2x−y+3z=8; −x+2y+z=4; 3x+y−4z=0 are consistent and, if consistent, solve.
   (b) Find characteristic roots and characteristic vectors of A=[[1,4],[2,3]].
3. (a) Particle x=2t²,y=t²−4t,z=3t−5. Find components of velocity and acceleration at t=1 in direction i−3j+2k.
   (b) Prove (i) ∇×(∇φ)=0; (ii) ∇·(∇×A⃗)=0.
   (c) Find tangent plane and normal line to xyz²=4 at (2,2,1).
4. (a) If F⃗=4xzi−y²j+yzk, evaluate flux over cube x=0,1;y=0,1;z=0,1.
   (b) State and prove Stoke’s theorem.

### Section B

5. (a) Obtain DE corresponding to y²=4a(x+a).
   (b) If Mdx+Ndy=0 is homogeneous, show y=vx transforms it into separable equation in v,x.
   (c) Solve x³dx+(y+1)²dy=0, x=1,y=2.
6. (a) Solve L di/dt+Ri=E sin2t, L,R,E constants, i=0 at t=0.
   (b) Find general solution dy/dx+(1/3)y=(1/3)(1−2x)y⁴, y(0)=1.
7. Solve (i)(D²−5D+6)y=e^(3x)+xe^(2x); (ii)(D³−2D+4)y=e^xcos x; (iii)(D²−D+1)y=sin2x; D=d/dx.
8. (a) Damped forced vibration d²x/dt²+2k dx/dt+b²x=e^(−kt)sinωt; solve for cases ω²≠b²−k² and ω²=b²−k². (b) Variation of parameters: x²y''+xy'−y=x²e^x.
