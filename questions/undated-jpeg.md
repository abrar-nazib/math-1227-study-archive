# Math 1227 Questions — Undated JPEG Pages

### Q.1

(a) Determine whether the vectors \(\vec A=2\hat i-\hat j+\hat k,\ \vec B=\hat i+3\hat j-2\hat k,\ \vec C=-2\hat i+\hat j-3\hat k,\ \vec D=3\hat i+2\hat j+5\hat k\) are linearly dependent or independent.

(b) Find the directional derivatives of \(\phi=x^2yz+4xz^2\) at \((1,-2,-1)\) in the direction \(2\hat i-\hat j-2\hat k\).

(c) Find the work done in moving an object along a straight line from \((3,2,-1)\) to \((2,-1,4)\) in a force field given by \(\vec F=4\hat i-3\hat j+2\hat k\).

### Q.2

(a) If \(\vec F=(2x+y)\hat i+(3y-x)\hat j\), evaluate \(\int_C\vec F\cdot d\vec r\) where \(C\) is in the \(xy\) plane consisting of the straight line from \((0,0)\) to \((2,0)\) and then to \((3,2)\).

(b) Evaluate \(\iint_S(\nabla\cdot\vec F)\cdot\hat n\,dS\), if \(\vec F=(x+2y)\hat i-2xy\hat j+x\hat k\) and \(S\) is the surface of \(2x+y+2z=6\), bounded by \(x=0,y=0,z=0\).

### Q.3

(a) State and prove the divergence theorem.

(b) Verify Stoke’s theorem for \(\vec F=(x+y)\hat i+(2x-z)\hat j+(y+z)\hat k\) taken over the triangle \(ABC\) cut off from the plane \(3x+2y+z=6\) by the coordinate planes.

### Q.4

(a) Define the following with examples and properties: i) Product of two matrices ii) Hermitian matrix iii) Skew symmetric matrix.

(b) Find the adjoint and inverse (if possible) of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&4&3\\1&3&3\\1&2&5\end{pmatrix}\).

(c) Show that \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-2&-4\\-1&3&4\\1&-2&-3\end{pmatrix}\) is an idempotent matrix.

### Q.5

(a) Find the eigen values and the corresponding eigen vectors of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&-2\\-1&2&1\\0&1&-1\end{pmatrix}\).

(b) Verify Cayley-Hamilton theorem for \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1&1\\-1&2&-1\\1&-1&2\end{pmatrix}\) and hence find \(A^{-1}\).

### Q.6

(a) Define ordinary differential equation with example. Find the equation of the curve which satisfies \((1+y^2)dx-xy\,dy=0\) and passes through the point \((1,0)\).

(b) Solve the initial value problem \((y+\sqrt{x^2+y^2})dx-xdy=0,\ y(1)=0\).

(c) Define integrating factor. Find the integrating factor of \(y\,dx-x\,dy=0\).

### Q.7

(a) Solve \(\frac{dy}{dx}-2y\tan x=y^2\tan^2x\).

(b) Solve: (i) \(\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+y=\sin2x\); (ii) \(\frac{d^3y}{dx^3}-3\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+3\frac{dy}{dx}-y=xe^x+e^x\).

### Q.8

(a) Use the method of variation of parameters. Solve \(\frac{d^3y}{dx^3}+2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-\frac{dy}{dx}-2y=e^x+x^2\).

(b) A circuit has an inductance of 0.05 Henry, a resistance of 20 ohms, a condenser of capacitance 100 microfarads, and an emf of \(E=100\) volts. Find \(q\) given the initial conditions \(q=0\) when \(t=0\).
