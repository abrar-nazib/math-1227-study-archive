# Math Questions — 2016

### Section A

1. (a) What is matrix and orthogonal matrix? Prove [[cosθ,sinθ],[−sinθ,cosθ]] is orthogonal.
   (b) If A=[[1,−1,1],[2,−1,0],[1,0,0]], find A² and show A²=A⁻¹.
   (c) What is Jacobian matrix? If T(u,v)=(u²−v²,2uv): (i) find velocity of u(t)=(t,t²) when t=1; (ii) find Jacobian of T(u,v).
2. (a) Reduce C=[[1,2,1,2],[1,3,2,2],[2,4,3,4],[3,7,4,6]] to echelon form and find rank.
   (b) Test consistency and solve x+y−z=1; 2x+3y−3z=3; x−3y+3z=2.
   (c) Find eigen values/vectors of D=[[1,1],[−2,4]].
3. (a) Find unit vector perpendicular to plane of A=2i−6j−3k and B=4i+3j−k.
   (b) Directional derivative of φ=x²yz+4xz² at (1,−2,−1) in direction 2i−j−2k.
   (c) If V⃗=(x+3y)i+(y−2z)j+(x+az)k, determine a so divergence is zero.
4. (a) Verify Green’s theorem for ∮C{(xy−x²)dx+x²ydy}, C perimeter bounded x=1,y=0,y=x.
   (b) If F⃗=4xzi−y²j+yzk, evaluate ∬S F⃗·n̂ds, cube x=0,1; y=0,1; z=0,1.

### Section B

5. (a) Solve (x²+y²)²dy/dx=a².
   (b) Define exact DE. Solve y(1+xy)dx=x(1−xy)dy=0.
   (c) Find singular and complete solution of y=px+p−p², p=dy/dx.
6. (a) What is DE? Mention three applications.
   (b) Obtain DE associated with primitive x²y³+x³y⁵=C.
   (c) Solve dy/dx+(1/3)y=(1/3)(1−2x)y⁴, y(0)=1.
7. Find general solution: (a)(D²−9D+18)y=e^(−3x); (b)(D²−2D+3)y=x³+sin x; (c)(D²+4)y=x²sin2x.
8. (a) Population doubles in 50 years; when will it treble, assuming rate proportional to population? (b) A body falls from rest with resistance proportional to v²; terminal velocity 50 ms⁻¹. Find velocity after 2 seconds.
