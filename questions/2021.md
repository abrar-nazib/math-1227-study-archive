# Math Questions — 2021

### Section A

1. (a) Determine whether r₁=2i−j+k, r₂=i+3j−2k, r₃=−2i+j−3k, r₄=3i+2j+5k are linearly dependent/independent.
   (b) Directional derivative of φ=4xz³−3x²y²z at (2,−1,2), direction 2i−3j+6k.
   (c) Show F⃗=xi+yj+zk is conservative. Find scalar potential φ, F⃗=∇φ.
2. (a) Find work moving particle once around circle C in xy plane, centre origin/radius z [as printed], force F⃗=y³i−x³j.
   (b) If F⃗=2yi−zj+x²k and S is paraboloid y²=8x in first octant, bounded y=4,z=6, evaluate ∬S F⃗·n̂ds.
3. (a) State and prove Stoke’s theorem.
   (b) Verify divergence theorem for A⃗=4xi−2y²j+z²k over region bounded x²+y²=4,z=0,z=3.
4. (a) What is matrix? Show every real symmetric matrix is Hermition. [sic]
   (b) What is inverse? Show inverse is unique.
   (c) Find adjoint/hence inverse of [[1,2,1],[5,2,3],[1,1,2]].

### Section B

5. (a) Define rank. Reduce A=[[6,1,3,8],[4,2,6,−1],[10,3,9,7],[16,4,12,15]] into normal form and find rank.
   (b) Find eigen values/vectors of [[3,2,4],[2,0,2],[4,2,3]].
6. (a) What is exact DE? Find condition for a DE to be exact.
   (b) Obtain DE associated with primitive y=Cx²+C².
   (c) Solve (x²+1)sin y dx+x cos y dy=0.
7. Solve: (a)(D²−4D+3)y=3e^x cosx+2xe^(3x); (b)(D²+5D+4)y=3−2x; (c)(D²−3D+2)y=sin3x.
8. (a) Solve (x²D²−3xD+4)y=x²; y(1)=0,y'(e)=e², D=d/dx.
   (b) Variation of parameters: y'''+2y''−y'−2y=e^x+x².
