# Math 1227 Questions: 2022–2024 and Undated JPEG Pages

## 2022

### Q.1

(a) Forces of magnitudes 5 and 3 units acting in the direction \(6\hat i+2\hat j+3\hat k\) and \(3\hat i-2\hat j+6\hat k\) respectively act on a particle which is displaced from the point \((2,2,-1)\) to \((4,3,1)\). Find the work done by the forces.

(b) In what direction from the point \((1,3,2)\) is the directional derivative of \(\phi=2xz-y^2\) a maximum? What is the magnitude of this maximum?

(c) Show that \(\vec F=(2xy+z^3)\hat i+x^2\hat j+3xz^2\hat k\) is a conservative force field. Also, find the scalar potential.

### Q.2

(a) If \(\vec F=(2x+y)\hat i+(3y-x)\hat j\), then evaluate \(\int_C\vec F\cdot d\vec r\) where \(C\) is the curve in the \(xy\) plane consisting of the straight lines from \((0,0)\) to \((2,0)\) and then to \((3,2)\).

(b) Evaluate \(\iint_S\phi\hat n\,dS\) where \(\phi=\frac{3}{8}xyz\) and \(S\) is the surface of the cylinder \(x^2+y^2=16\) included in the first octant between \(z=0\) and \(z=5\).

### Q.3

(a) State and prove divergence theorem.

(b) Verify Green’s theorem in the plane for \(\oint_C(3x^2-8y^2)dx+(4y-6xy)dy\), where \(C\) is the boundary of the region defined by \(y=\sqrt{x}\) and \(y=x^2\).

### Q.4

(a) Define symmetric and skew-Hermitian matrix with example.

(b) Show that every square matrix can be uniquely expressed as the sum of a symmetric and skew-symmetric matrices.

(c) Define rank of a matrix. Reduce the matrix \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2&3&-1&-1\\1&-1&-2&-4\\3&1&3&-2\\6&3&0&-7\end{pmatrix}\) into normal form and hence find its rank.

### Q.5

(a) Determine for what values of \(\lambda\) and \(\mu\) the system \(x+y+z=6\), \(x+2y+3z=10\), \(x+2y+\lambda z=\mu\) has: (i) no solution, (ii) unique solution, (iii) infinite number of solutions.

(b) Find the eigen values and the corresponding eigen vectors of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&-1\\1&2&1\\2&2&3\end{pmatrix}\).

### Q.6

(a) Define a differential equation. Give examples. Mention some applications of differential equations.

(b) Solve the initial value problem: \(x\sin y\,dx+(x^2+1)\cos y\,dy=0,\ y(0)=\frac{\pi}{2}\).

(c) Find the constant \(A\) such that \(\left(\frac{Ay}{x^3}+\frac{y}{x^2}\right)dx+\left(\frac{1}{x^2}-\frac{1}{x}\right)dy=0\) is exact and solve the resulting equation.

### Q.7

(a) Identify and solve \(xy-\frac{dy}{dx}=y^3e^{-x^2}\).

(b) Find the complete solution of \(y''-6y'+25y=0;\ y(0)=-3,\ y'(0)=-1\).

### Q.8

Solve completely: (a) \((D^2-4)y=e^x-\sin x\); (b) \((D^2-4D+4)y=x^2+e^x\); (c) \((D^2+1)y=x\sin x\).

## 2023

### Q.1

(a) Are the vectors \(\vec A=2\hat i-\hat k\), \(\vec B=3\hat i-2\hat j+\hat k\), and \(\vec C=3\hat i+2\hat j-\hat k\) linearly independent? If not, find a dependent relation between them.

(b) Find the unit tangent vector at the point where \(t=2\) on the curve \(x=t^2+1,\ y=4t-3,\ z=2t^2-6t\).

(c) Find the curvature \(k\) for the curve \(x=t,\ y=t^2,\ z=\frac{2t^3}{3}\).

### Q.2

(a) Write down the geometrical interpretation of \(\nabla\phi\).

(b) Evaluate \(\int_C\vec A\cdot d\vec r\) along the curve \(x^2+y^2=1,\ z=1\), in the positive direction from \((0,1,1)\) to \((1,0,1)\), if \(\vec A=(yz+zx)\hat i+xz\hat j+(xy+2z)\hat k\).

### Q.3

(a) Evaluate \(\iint_S\phi\hat n\,dS\) where \(\phi=4x+3y-2z\) and \(S\) is the surface of \(2x+y+2z=6\) bounded by \(x=0,x=1,y=0,y=2\).

(b) Verify Stokes theorem for \(\vec A=3y\hat i-xz\hat j+yz^2\hat k\), where \(s\) is the surface of the paraboloid \(2z=x^2+y^2\) bounded by \(z=2\) and \(c\) is its boundary traversed in the clockwise direction.

### Q.4

(a) Define matrix and symmetric matrix. If \(A\) and \(B\) are \(n\)-squared symmetric matrices then prove that \(AB\) is symmetric if and only if \(A\) and \(B\) are commutative matrices.

(b) Given \(A=\begin{pmatrix}11&-25\\4&-9\end{pmatrix}\), prove that \(A^n=\begin{pmatrix}1+10n&-25n\\4n&1-10n\end{pmatrix}\).

(c) Show that \(A=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\alpha&\sin\alpha\\-\sin\alpha&\cos\alpha\end{pmatrix}\) is orthogonal.

### Q.5

(a) For what values of \(\lambda\), the system \(x+y+z=1\), \(x+2y+4z=\lambda\), \(x+4y+10z=\lambda^2\) has a solution. Solve them completely in each case.

(b) Define eigen value and eigen vector. Find the eigen value and corresponding eigen vectors of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&-3&3\\3&-5&3\\6&-6&4\end{pmatrix}\).

### Q.6

(a) Define exact differential equation. Test whether \((x+y)^2dx-(y^2-2xy-x^2)dy=0\) is exact and hence solve it.

(b) Form a differential equation of \(y=a\ln x+b\).

(c) Solve \(\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{xy-2y^2}{x^2-3xy}\).

### Q.7

(a) Define an integrating factor. Explain it with an example.

(b) Find an integrating factor and hence solve \(y\ln x\,dx+(x-\ln y)dy=0\).

(c) Identify and solve \((1+x^2)\frac{dy}{dx}+2xy=4x^2;\ y(0)=1\).

### Q.8

Solve: (a) \((D^2+a^2)y=\tan ax\); (b) \((D^3-D^2-6D)y=x^2+1\); (c) \((D^4+D^2+1)y=ax^2+be^{-x}\sin2x\).

## 2024

### Q.1

(a) Find an equation for the plane perpendicular to \(\vec A=2\hat i+3\hat j+6\hat k\) and passing through the terminal point to the vector \(\vec B=\hat i+5\hat j+3\hat k\).

(b) Show that \(\vec E=\frac{\vec r}{r^2}\) is conservative. Find a scalar function \(\phi\) such that \(\vec E=-\nabla\phi\) and \(\phi(a)=0\), where \(a>0\).

(c) Evaluate \(\int_C\vec A\cdot d\vec r\) along the curve \(x^2+y^2=1,\ Z=1\) in the positive direction of \((0,1,1)\) to \((1,0,1)\) if \(\vec A=(yz+zx)\hat i+xz\hat j+(xy+2z)\hat k\).

### Q.2

(a) Evaluate \(\iint_S(\nabla\times\vec F)\cdot\hat n\,ds\) where \(\vec F=(x+2y)\hat i-3z\hat j+x\hat k\) and \(S\) is the surface of \(2x+y+2z=6\) bounded by \(x=0,x=1,y=0,y=2\).

(b) If \(\vec F=(2x^2-3z)\hat i-2xy\hat j-4x\hat k\), then evaluate \(\iiint_V(\nabla\times\vec F)dV\) where \(V\) is the closed region bounded by \(x=0,y=0,z=0,2x+2y+z=4\).

### Q.3

(a) State and prove divergence theorem.

(b) Verify Stoke’s theorem for \(\vec F=xz\hat i-y\hat j+x^2yz\hat k\), where \(s\) is the surface of the region bounded by \(x=0,y=0,z=0,2x+y+2z=8\) which is not included in the \(xz\) plane.

### Q.4

(a) What is the adjoint of a matrix? Prove that the adjoint of a symmetric matrix is itself symmetric.

(b) Find the symmetric and skew symmetric part of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2&3&4\\4&3&1\\1&2&4\end{pmatrix}\).

(c) Find the Hermitian part of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2+3i&1-i&2+i\\3&4+3i&5\\1&1+i&2i\end{pmatrix}\).

### Q.5

(a) Find the eigen values and the corresponding eigen vectors of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&-2\\-1&2&1\\0&1&-1\end{pmatrix}\).

(b) Solve by matrix method: \(x-y+2z=3\), \(x+2y+3z=5\), \(3x-4y-5z=-13\).

### Q.6

(a) Define rank of a matrix. Reduce \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2&3&-1&-1\\1&-1&-2&-4\\3&1&3&-2\\6&3&0&-7\end{pmatrix}\) into normal form and hence find its rank.

(b) Define differential equation. Also mention the uses of differential equation.

(c) Solve \((y^2e^{xy^2}+4x^3)dx+(2xye^{xy^2}-3y^2)dy=0\).

### Q.7

(a) What is exact differential equation? Find the general solution of \((12y+4x^3+6x^2)dx+3(x+xy^2)dy=0\).

(b) Solve \(\frac{dy}{dx}+x\sin2y=x^3\cos^2y\).

(c) Find the integrating factor and hence find the general solution of \(y\ln y\,dx+(x-\ln y)dy=0\).

### Q.8

(a) Solve \((x^3D^3+2xD-2)y=x^2\ln x+3x\).

(b) Find the current \(i\) from \(L\frac{di}{dt}+Ri=E\sin2t\), where \(L,R,E\) are constants subject to \(i=0\) when \(t=0\).

## Undated JPEG Questions

### Q.1

(a) Determine whether the vectors \(\vec A=2\hat i-\hat j+\hat k,\ \vec B=\hat i+3\hat j-2\hat k,\ \vec C=-2\hat i+\hat j-3\hat k,\ \vec D=3\hat i+2\hat j+5\hat k\) are linearly dependent or independent.

(b) Find the directional derivatives of \(\phi=x^2yz+4xz^2\) at \((1,-2,-1)\) in the direction \(2\hat i-\hat j-2\hat k\).

(c) Find the work done in moving an object along a straight line from \((3,2,-1)\) to \((2,-1,4)\) in a force field given by \(\vec F=4\hat i-3\hat j+2\hat k\).

### Q.2

(a) If \(\vec F=(2x+y)\hat i+(3y-x)\hat j\), evaluate \(\int_C\vec F\cdot d\vec r\) where \(C\) is in the \(xy\) plane consisting of the straight line from \((0,0)\) to \((2,0)\) and then to \((3,2)\).

(b) Evaluate \(\iint_S(\nabla\cdot\vec F)\cdot\hat n\,dS\), if \(\vec F=(x+2y)\hat i-2xy\hat j+x\hat k\) and \(S\) is the surface of \(2x+y+2z=6\), bounded by \(x=0,y=0,z=0\).

### Q.3

(a) State and prove the divergence theorem.

(b) Verify Stoke’s theorem for \(\vec F=(x+y)\hat i+(2x-z)\hat j+(y+z)\hat k\) taken over the triangle \(ABC\) cut off from the plane \(3x+2y+z=6\) by the coordinate planes.

### Q.4

(a) Define the following with examples and properties: i) Product of two matrices ii) Hermitian matrix iii) Skew symmetric matrix.

(b) Find the adjoint and inverse (if possible) of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&4&3\\1&3&3\\1&2&5\end{pmatrix}\).

(c) Show that \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-2&-4\\-1&3&4\\1&-2&-3\end{pmatrix}\) is an idempotent matrix.

### Q.5

(a) Find the eigen values and the corresponding eigen vectors of \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&-2\\-1&2&1\\0&1&-1\end{pmatrix}\).

(b) Verify Cayley-Hamilton theorem for \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1&1\\-1&2&-1\\1&-1&2\end{pmatrix}\) and hence find \(A^{-1}\).

### Q.6

(a) Define ordinary differential equation with example. Find the equation of the curve which satisfies \((1+y^2)dx-xy\,dy=0\) and passes through the point \((1,0)\).

(b) Solve the initial value problem \((y+\sqrt{x^2+y^2})dx-xdy=0,\ y(1)=0\).

(c) Define integrating factor. Find the integrating factor of \(y\,dx-x\,dy=0\).

### Q.7

(a) Solve \(\frac{dy}{dx}-2y\tan x=y^2\tan^2x\).

(b) Solve: (i) \(\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+y=\sin2x\); (ii) \(\frac{d^3y}{dx^3}-3\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+3\frac{dy}{dx}-y=xe^x+e^x\).

### Q.8

(a) Use the method of variation of parameters. Solve \(\frac{d^3y}{dx^3}+2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-\frac{dy}{dx}-2y=e^x+x^2\).

(b) A circuit has an inductance of 0.05 Henry, a resistance of 20 ohms, a condenser of capacitance 100 microfarads, and an emf of \(E=100\) volts. Find \(q\) given the initial conditions \(q=0\) when \(t=0\).
